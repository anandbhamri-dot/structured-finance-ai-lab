Cash flow detail

Waterfall order each period: fees, class interest, then principal, following each class's pay rule. Sequential classes are paid one after another in the order listed; consecutive pro-rata classes in the same pay group are paid pari passu, sharing interest shortfalls, principal and writedowns by balance. After the stepdown month, while the cumulative loss trigger passes, principal can either go pro-rata to all classes or keep the class rules; a breach always reverts to the class rules and locks the OC target. Excess spread covers losses and builds OC before any release to the residual. Defaulted loans stop paying interest, stay in the collateral balance until liquidation after the recovery lag, and the loss is realised at liquidation. There is no servicer advancing, fee and interest shortfalls are tracked but not carried forward, and each collateral loan is projected individually.
