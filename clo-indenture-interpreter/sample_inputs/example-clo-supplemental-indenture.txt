FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT

This is not a real supplemental indenture. It was written from scratch for the CLO Indenture
Interpreter, to show a deal whose terms are spread over more than one document. Every name, figure
and date in it is made up. Read it together with the fictional sample indenture for Example CLO I,
listed above the indenture, so that its amendments take priority over the original definitions.

FIRST SUPPLEMENTAL INDENTURE

made on March 2, 2009

among

Example CLO I, Ltd., as Issuer,

Example CLO I, LLC, as Co-Issuer,

and

Example Trust Company, N.A., as Trustee

supplementing the Indenture made on August 14, 2007 among the same parties

WHEREAS the Collateral Manager has asked the Issuer to relax two of the Collateral Quality Tests
in light of the tighter spreads and the smaller number of obligors available in the loan market;

WHEREAS the Holders of a majority of the Controlling Class have consented to the amendments below;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows.

Section 1. Amendments to Section 1.1 of the Indenture. With effect from the date of this
Supplemental Indenture, the following definitions in Section 1.1 of the Indenture are amended and
restated in full:

"Minimum Diversity Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the Diversity Score is
at least 45.

"Minimum Weighted Average Spread Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the
Weighted Average Spread is at least 2.00%.

Section 2. Effect of this Supplemental Indenture. Except as amended above, the Indenture remains in
full force and effect, and each reference in it to the Indenture is a reference to the Indenture as
supplemented by this Supplemental Indenture.

Section 3. Governing Law. This Supplemental Indenture is governed by the law of the State of New
York.
