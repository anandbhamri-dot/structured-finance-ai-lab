ARTICLE I
DEFINITIONS

Section 1.1 Definitions. In this Indenture, the following terms have the meanings given below.
Words in the singular include the plural and the other way round.

"Administrative Expenses": The fees, expenses and indemnities owed by the Issuer or the Co-Issuer
to the Trustee, the Collateral Administrator, the Administrator, the Rating Agencies, accountants,
counsel and any other service provider, paid in the order set out in this definition: first to the
Trustee and the Collateral Administrator, then to the Rating Agencies, and then to all others pro
rata.

"Aggregate Principal Balance": When used for all or part of the Collateral Obligations, the sum of
their principal balances on the date of determination.

"Business Day": Any day on which commercial banks in New York City are open for business.

"CCC Collateral Obligation": A Collateral Obligation (other than a Defaulted Obligation) rated
"Caa1" or below by Moody's or "CCC+" or below by S&P.

"CCC Excess": The amount by which the Aggregate Principal Balance of all CCC Collateral Obligations
exceeds 8.0% of the Collateral Principal Amount. The CCC Collateral Obligations with the lowest
Market Value make up the CCC Excess, and each of them is carried in the Principal Collateral Value
at the lesser of 70% of its principal balance and its Market Value.

"Class A/B Interest Coverage Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the Class
A/B Interest Coverage Ratio on that date is at least 118.00%. The Interest Coverage Tests apply from
the Determination Date for the second Payment Date.

"Class A/B Overcollateralization Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the
Class A/B Overcollateralization Ratio on that date is at least 112.00%.

"Class C Interest Coverage Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the Class C
Interest Coverage Ratio on that date is at least 113.00%.

"Class C Overcollateralization Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the Class
C Overcollateralization Ratio on that date is at least 107.00%.

Page 3
"Class D Interest Coverage Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the Class D
Interest Coverage Ratio on that date is at least 108.00%.

"Class D Overcollateralization Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the Class
D Overcollateralization Ratio on that date is at least 104.50%.

"Class E Interest Coverage Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the Class E
Interest Coverage Ratio on that date is at least 104.00%.

"Class E Overcollateralization Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the Class
E Overcollateralization Ratio on that date is at least 102.00%.

"Closing Date": August 14, 2007.

"Collateral Principal Amount": On any date, the Aggregate Principal Balance of the Collateral
Obligations plus the Principal Proceeds held in the Collection Account.

"Coverage Tests": The Overcollateralization Tests and the Interest Coverage Tests.

"Defaulted Obligation": A Collateral Obligation on which a payment default has occurred and is
continuing, or whose obligor is the subject of a bankruptcy proceeding. A Defaulted Obligation is
carried in the Principal Collateral Value at the lesser of its Market Value and its recovery rate,
and at zero once the Issuer has held it as a Defaulted Obligation for three years.

"Discount Obligation": A loan bought for less than 80% of its principal balance (or less than 85% if
it is rated below "B3" by Moody's), or any other Collateral Obligation bought for less than 75% (or
less than 80% if rated below "B3"). It is carried at its purchase price until its Market Value has
stayed above 90% for 30 consecutive days.

"Effective Date": The earlier of (i) December 27, 2007 and (ii) the first day on which the Aggregate
Principal Balance of the Collateral Obligations bought by the Issuer reaches the Target Par Amount.

"Fee Basis Amount": On any Payment Date, the Collateral Principal Amount at the start of the related
Collection Period, with Defaulted Obligations counted at their principal balance.

Page 4
"Incentive Management Fee": The fee payable to the Collateral Manager on each Payment Date once the
Holders of the Subordinated Notes have received an Internal Rate of Return equal to 11%, equal to
20% of any remaining Interest Proceeds and Principal Proceeds after the Subordinated Notes have
received that return.

"Interest Reinvestment Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date during the
Reinvestment Period if the Class E Overcollateralization Ratio on that date is at least 102.80%.

"Market Value": The bid price of a Collateral Obligation obtained from a recognised pricing service,
as a percentage of its principal balance.

"Maximum Weighted Average Rating Factor Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if
the Weighted Average Rating Factor does not exceed the number set out in the Collateral Quality
Matrix in Schedule 1 for the row and column the Collateral Manager has chosen, adjusted by the
Moody's Weighted Average Recovery Adjustment.

"Minimum Diversity Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the Diversity Score is
at least 48.

"Minimum Weighted Average Coupon Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the
Weighted Average Coupon of the fixed rate Collateral Obligations is at least 8.00%.

"Minimum Weighted Average Spread Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the
Weighted Average Spread is at least 2.10%.

"Moody's Minimum Weighted Average Recovery Rate Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement
Date if the Moody's Weighted Average Recovery Rate is at least 44.0%.

"Non-Call Period": The period from the Closing Date to but excluding July 18, 2011, during which the
Notes may not be redeemed at the option of the Holders of the Subordinated Notes.

"Payment Date": The 18th day of January, April, July and October in each year (or the next Business
Day if that day is not a Business Day), commencing in January 2008, and the Stated Maturity.

Page 5
"Principal Collateral Value": On any Measurement Date, the Aggregate Principal Balance of the
Collateral Obligations, except that Defaulted Obligations, Discount Obligations and the CCC Excess
are carried at the values given in their definitions, plus the Principal Proceeds in the Collection
Account.

"Reinvestment Period": The period from the Closing Date to and including the earliest of (i) July
18, 2014, (ii) the date on which the Notes are accelerated and (iii) the date the Collateral Manager
gives notice that it can no longer reinvest.

"S&P CDO Monitor Test": A test run on S&P's CDO Monitor model; its result is given to the Trustee by
the Collateral Manager.

"Senior Collateral Management Fee": The fee payable to the Collateral Manager on each Payment Date,
equal to (i) 0.075% per annum of the Fee Basis Amount for each Payment Date up to and including the
Payment Date in July 2011 and (ii) 0.15% per annum of the Fee Basis Amount after that, computed on an
Actual/360 basis.

"Senior Deferred Structuring Fee": The fee payable to the Collateral Manager equal to 0.08% per annum
of the Fee Basis Amount on each Payment Date on or prior to the Payment Date in July 2011.

"Subordinated Collateral Management Fee": The fee payable to the Collateral Manager on each Payment
Date equal to 0.30% per annum of the Fee Basis Amount, computed on an Actual/360 basis. The
Collateral Manager may elect to defer all or part of it.

"Target Par Amount": U.S.$400,000,000.

"Weighted Average Life Test": A test that is satisfied on any Measurement Date if the weighted
average life of the Collateral Obligations is on or before 9.5 years minus the time elapsed since
the Effective Date.

ARTICLE V
EVENTS OF DEFAULT

Section 5.1 Events of Default. Each of the following is an "Event of Default":

(a) a failure to pay interest on the Class A Notes or the Class B Notes when due, continuing for
five Business Days;

(b) a failure to pay principal of any Note at its Stated Maturity;

(c) the Issuer becoming an investment company required to register under the Investment Company Act;

(d) a material breach of a covenant of the Issuer, not cured within 30 days of notice; and

(e) on any Measurement Date that the Class A Overcollateralization Ratio is less than 101.00%.

REDEMPTION

Section 9.2 Optional Redemption. After the Non-Call Period, the Holders of a majority of the
Subordinated Notes may direct the Issuer to redeem the Notes in whole on any Payment Date, from the

ARTICLE XII
SALE OF COLLATERAL OBLIGATIONS; PURCHASE OF ADDITIONAL COLLATERAL OBLIGATIONS

Section 12.1 Eligibility Criteria. Each Collateral Obligation must, when bought, be a loan or bond
denominated in U.S. Dollars, pay interest at least semi-annually, and mature no later than the
Stated Maturity, except that up to 2% of the Collateral Principal Amount may mature after it.

Section 12.2 Concentration Limitations.

"Concentration Limitations": Limitations that are satisfied on any Measurement Date if, in the
aggregate, the Collateral Obligations comply with all of the following, each measured as a
percentage of the Collateral Principal Amount:

(i) not more than 10.0% may consist of Collateral Obligations that pay interest less frequently
than quarterly;

(ii) not more than 10.0% may consist of Revolving Collateral Obligations and Delayed Drawdown
Collateral Obligations;

(iii) not more than 5.0% may consist of Fixed Rate Collateral Obligations;

(iv) not less than 95.0% may consist of Senior Secured Loans or Second Lien Loans;

(v) not more than 10.0% may consist of Second Lien Loans, Senior Unsecured Loans and High-Yield
Bonds;

(vi) not more than 15.0% may consist of Participation Interests;

(vii) not more than 2.0% may be issued by any single obligor, except that up to four obligors may
each represent up to 2.5%;

Page 15
(viii) not more than 10.0% may be issued by obligors in any one S&P Industry Classification, except
that up to two industries may each represent up to 12.0%;

(ix) not more than 8.0% may consist of Collateral Obligations rated "Caa1" or below by Moody's or
"CCC+" or below by S&P;

(x) not more than 5.0% may consist of DIP Collateral Obligations;

(xi) not more than 5.0% may consist of Current Pay Obligations;

(xii) not more than 5.0% may consist of PIK Obligations;

(xiii) not more than 5.0% may be issued by obligors whose total potential indebtedness under their
loan agreements is less than U.S.$100,000,000;

(xiv) not more than 2.0% may consist of Zero Coupon Obligations;

(xv) not more than 25.0% may consist of Cov-Lite Loans;

(xvi) not more than 20.0% may consist of Synthetic Securities;

(xvii) not more than 20.0% may be issued by obligors Domiciled outside the United States;

(xviii) not more than 15.0% may be issued by Canadian obligors;

(xix) not more than 5.0% may consist of Senior Unsecured Loans; and

(xx) not more than 5.0% may consist of bonds.
